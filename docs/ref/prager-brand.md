# Prager Brothers brand (Phase 0 scrape)

Source: pragerbrothers.com (Playwright computed-style capture).

## Business
Artisan/sourdough bakery, Carlsbad CA. Bread + pastries + wood-fired pizza + coffee. Retail + online order/pickup. Multiple SoCal locations. Runs a real **Music Calendar** (live music events) — the vinyl section is on-brand.

## Brand colors (from computed styles)
- **Primary blue (peacock/teal):** `rgb(0,127,163)` = **`#007FA3`** — nav links, buttons, logo badge.
- Body grey: `#616161` / `#656565`.
- Background: white `#FFFFFF` on the live site (cream is our editorial addition per the brief "cream + sky blue").

Our reskin uses cream `#F7F0E6` as the surface + `#007FA3` as the accent, per the cember warm-cream direction + Prager blue brief.
